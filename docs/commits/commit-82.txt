no 20:00 UTC projection, the target hour at RNO and KSFO. Open-Meteo's
Previous Runs API is a shallow second route from 2024 for most models,
rejects 850 hPa temperature at every offset and has no working GEFS
route. No public GFS v17 retrospective forecast runs were found.
