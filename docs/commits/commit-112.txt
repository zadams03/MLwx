Session 112: D106 analysis plan and a descriptive analysis of the stage C curve and its daily maximum (F155)

D106 records the owner's decisions from the statistics planning chat: F154
accepted, and a plan for a descriptive analysis of the stage C curve and its
daily maximum on spent data, fixed before any of its numbers was computed
(data, references, metrics, breakdowns with bin edges from training rows,
the daily maximum as the main section, curve shape, drift, model internals,
limits, uncertainty and formal tests, the ZUCK case, outputs, and the next
two sessions). It changes no verdict, model, setting, rule, bar or table,
and nothing in D73, D77.6 or D105.5. Stage C's look was not run again.

F155: the stage C predictions at the 12 airports of D103.4 were regenerated
through the record's own routes and reproduce session 104's and session
107's files exactly (Gate D 9,936 cells and 6,570 days; Gate C 11,100 score
rows and 4,374 days). Seven forecasts were compared: raw GFS, persistence,
climatology, constant bias, linear MOS, the baseline and the curve. The
curve beats raw GFS at 12 of 12 airports for both outputs and linear MOS at
11 of 12 hourly (Diebold-Mariano with Benjamini-Hochberg), and the baseline
at 4 of 12, all development airports. The daily maximum reads low by +0.12
to +0.25 degrees C at every claim airport; this is consistent with a
selection effect but not separated from any damping of the forecast peak.
The curve is 1.5 to 3.3 times as jagged as raw GFS across leads.

for later; nothing was changed on them. Figures are in figures/analysis/
