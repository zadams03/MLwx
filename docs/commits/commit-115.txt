Session 115: D109 and F158, an observation plausibility check at the 12 stage C airports

D109 records the owner's decision to check the IEM hourly temperatures for implausible values before the IFS comparisons, after a read-only search found that the project had never done so: the readers reject non-finite and missing values and handle repeated timestamps, but any finite tmpc value was used as reported. D109 fixes four checks and their thresholds before any value was looked at (out of range, spike, stuck sensor, extreme residual against the median raw GFS), limits the session to flagging and counting, moves the IFS work of D108.7 to session 116, and notes for the planned health-check session that the repo uses a root layout, not the research/ and audit/ layout recorded in D90.5.

F158 reports the check on 562,232 usable hourly values at EGLC, LFPG, KDSM, YSDU, KRNO, KSFO, EDDM, KORD, CYYZ, ZGSZ, ZUCK and NZWN (2021-03-24 to 2026-07-31). The series built with the project's reader matches every stage C table row's observation and the usable daily-maximum days on record at 12 of 12 airports. 772 hours were flagged: none out of range, 1 spike, 666 stuck-sensor hours and 106 large residuals; 22 of 23,392 usable daily maxima are set by a flagged hour. The one spike (CYYZ 2025-04-28T01:00Z, 27.0 C between 15.0 and 11.0) looks like a bad report and sets the daily maximum of a claim-look-A day; its effect on any score was not computed, and stage C's verdicts stand (D101.2). The stuck runs all occur at airports reporting whole degrees and the residual flags cluster on cold pools at Reno, fronts at KORD and KDSM, and Dubbo's afternoon cool change, so both read as likely real weather, which the thresholds were not changed to exclude.

Nothing in data/raw/ or any existing processed file was changed, no value was removed or filled, no model was fitted and nothing was scored. The review correction (Dubbo, not Sydney, in STATUS) was applied before commit.
