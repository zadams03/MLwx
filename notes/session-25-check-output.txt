PART 0b - the station chosen: BZN (Bozeman), and why.
==========================================================================
Both candidates clear the basic bar equally on this 7-day sample: both
report hourly and completely, both have a genuine grid-elevation
mismatch that is REAL BUT SMALL rather than the 'hundreds of metres'
the session prompt flagged as possible (BZN -16 m, RNO -1 m - see
PART 0a). That is itself worth recording plainly: it says Open-Meteo's
~0.25-degree GFS grid cell, averaged over a BROAD valley floor at
either location, lands close to the valley's own elevation rather than
blending in nearby peaks - which is exactly what 'broad, not
pathological' should look like, and is the reason neither candidate
shows a dramatic mismatch the way a narrow canyon station (Aspen-style)
would.

Bozeman (BZN) sits in the Gallatin Valley at 1,364 m, a wide
agricultural valley many kilometres across, bounded by the Bridger
Range to the north and the Gallatin and Tobacco Root ranges to the
south and west - genuinely mountain-terrain-affected (elevation,
cold-air drainage, a real winter/summer diurnal range) without the
valley itself narrowing to a canyon. Reno (RNO) sits in the Truckee
Meadows at 1,345 m, but immediately to its west the Sierra Nevada
front rises very steeply to peaks above 3,000 m within about 20 km -
a sharper, more abrupt transition right at the edge of the valley than
Bozeman's more gradually-rising surrounding ranges. Both are
legitimate 'broad mountain-valley' choices per the session prompt;
Bozeman is chosen as the clearer case of the two - a wide valley
ringed by mountains at a comfortable distance, closer in shape to
what 'broad, not pathological' is meant to describe, and with no
single abrupt escarpment sitting right at the grid cell's edge the
way Reno's does.

Side by side with the four airports already in the project (SPEC 3.4):

  LFPG  lat 49.0153, lon 2.5344, elevation 109.0 m

  BZN is 7,359 km from EGLC, 1,482 km from DSM (the nearest of the four).
  BZN is by far the HIGHEST-ELEVATION airport in the project so far -
  1364 m against DSM's 294 m, the next highest.

PART 0c - the target hour: local standard noon, computed and checked
==========================================================================
The session prompt asks for the standard-time offset to be confirmed
from the station's actual timezone rather than assumed. This checks it
against the timezone database, the same way F32 did for DSM and F50
for Dubbo.

timezone from IEM metadata : America/Denver
  mid-summer  : 12:00 UTC = 06:00 MDT (UTC-6)
  mid-winter  : 12:00 UTC = 05:00 MST (UTC-7)
  standard-time offset (read from a January date, guaranteed standard time)  : UTC-7
  so local standard noon (12:00) = 19:00 UTC

Note on daylight saving: Montana observes it (MDT, Mar-Nov). The
convention (D27/D33/D37) uses the STANDARD offset (MST, UTC-7)
regardless, so the target stays one fixed UTC hour all year, per the
session prompt.

PART B6 - timezone check: is the tz=UTC request really UTC?
==========================================================================
Every IEM request in this project sends tz=UTC. The same window was
pulled a second time with tz=America/Denver, so the two can be
compared. June is Mountain DAYLIGHT time (MDT, UTC-6), not the
standard offset used for the target hour (MST, UTC-7) - this check
measures whichever offset is actually in force during the sample.

UTC-request file        : iem_asos_BZN_2024-06-01_2024-06-04_routine-tmpc-tmpf.csv
local-request file      : iem_asos_BZN_2024-06-01_2024-06-04_routine-localtime.csv
rows, UTC request       : 72   first 2024-06-01 00:56:00, last 2024-06-03 23:56:00
rows, local request     : 72   first 2024-06-01 00:56:00, last 2024-06-03 23:56:00

The two requests use the same start/end date strings but under
different tz, so they do not cover the same physical hours at their
edges. Each local-file timestamp is shifted by a candidate number of
hours and matched against the UTC file's timestamps directly, and the
temperature values are compared at matching physical instants.

  local + 9h        63                     5  (7.9%)

  best match: local time plus 6 hours lines up with the UTC
              series (100.0% of 66 comparable hours identical).
  Expected: America/Denver is on daylight saving in June (MDT, UTC-6),
