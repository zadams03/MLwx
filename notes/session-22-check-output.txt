-- THE FLIPPED-SEASON CHECK - bias grouped by DUBBO'S OWN season --
    Dubbo is Southern Hemisphere, so its physical seasons are six
    months out of phase with the calendar labels above. The model's
    season feature is day-of-year sine/cosine (D19) - pure calendar
    position, with no hemisphere knowledge built in. If Dubbo carries
    a calendar-driven bias the way CDG's did (F28), it should show up
    HERE, phase-shifted into Dubbo's own summer (Dec-Feb) rather than
    absent or copying the Northern shape. NOTE: the three-month
    buckets (DJF/MAM/JJA/SON) are the SAME calendar months as the
    table just above - only the SEASON NAME attached to each bucket
    changes, because Dubbo's own summer genuinely falls in Dec-Feb.
    The check is which bucket carries the largest bias, read against
    what that bucket means locally rather than in the North.
    Dubbo season           days  mean bias   st dev  mean |bias|
    Dubbo summer DJF        248     -0.973    1.638        1.408
    Dubbo autumn MAM        342     +0.073    1.635        1.135
    Dubbo winter JJA        334     +0.140    1.614        1.176
    Dubbo spring SON        269     +0.027    1.881        1.387

    Largest-magnitude seasonal bias, by Dubbo's own season: Dubbo summer DJF (-0.973 degC).
    The largest signal sits in DEC-FEB - Dubbo's own local summer,
    not the Northern airports' JJA summer. A model that had somehow
    baked in 'Northern summer is warm' would put its biggest swing
    near June-August; instead the biggest swing at Dubbo tracks
    Dubbo's own actual summer. The season_sin/season_cos features
    carry no hemisphere information at all, only calendar position
    (D19) - so this is evidence the underlying PHYSICAL bias is
    genuinely phase-shifted at Dubbo, and the model is free to fit
    whatever calendar position that local signal actually falls on.
