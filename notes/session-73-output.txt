2. Target and prediction.
   - Residual = observed temperature minus GRIB forecast temperature at the
     target hour (SPEC 4.2; D48.1). At RNO the forecast is the
     elevation-adjusted GRIB 2 m temperature (SPEC 5.2, 7.2; D48.3).
   - Corrected forecast = GRIB forecast + predicted residual (SPEC 4.2;
     D48.1; D21.2).
   - MAE is the mean absolute gap between the corrected forecast and the
     observation, in degC (SPEC 5.1; D21.9). Raw GFS: |obs - raw GFS|
     (SPEC 5.2). Persistence: |obs - previous calendar day's paired
     observation| (D21.8, D48.9, SPEC 2.1d).

3. Feature columns and order.
   - B (SPEC 8.2; D48.4): forecast_temp_c, season_sin, season_cos,
     cloud_cover, wind_speed_10m. Session 72 columns: temperature_grib_c,
     season_sin, season_cos, cloud_cover_grib_pct, wind_speed_grib_kmh.
   - B+D,L,R,T (SPEC 8.1; D58 items 1 and 3): B plus lapse_rate_t2_t850 (L),
     dewpoint_depression_t2m_floored (D), pressure_tendency_3h_hpa (T),
     dswrf_2h_wm2 (R). Order used: B's five, then L, D, T, R (gap G15).

4. D's floor. dewpoint_depression_t2m_floored = max(dewpoint_depression_t2m,
   0), applied in the feature matrix only; the unfloored column is kept
   (SPEC 8.1; D58 item 3; F100 Task 1 item 3; D53).

5. Complete-case rule. A row is used only if every relevant column has a
   value, built over B's columns and the columns backing D (unfloored), L,
   R and T (SPEC 8.3; D58 item 5). Rows with no paired observation are
   dropped and counted (SPEC 2.2, 4.5). Train 2021-03-24..2024-07-31, test
   2024-08-01..2025-07-31 (SPEC 8.3; D58 item 4).
   Persistence is scored only on test days with a previous-day observation;
   raw GFS and the models on every test day (SPEC 8.5; D58 item 6).

6. MAE definition and rounding. Mean absolute error in degC (SPEC 5.1).
   F109 reports each MAE at 4 decimals (DECISIONS-archive F109 Step 2;
   D64.4 "each MAE (at F109's 4 decimals)").
